# Terms

Review the changes and extract every unconventional or bespoke term used for abstract objects, processes, and concepts.

## Step 1 — the term file

Create a temporary markdown file in the working directory. For each term, record:

- the term
- its meaning
- why it was chosen
- some proposed alternative terms for it

## Step 2 — wait

Hand the file to the user and stop. They use it to confirm each term choice or provide their own custom term.

Do not incorporate anything until they have answered.

## Step 3 — incorporate

Apply their choices: rename the terms in the work, and use their words in your replies from then on.

Delete the temporary file once every term is resolved.

## Goal

Map your language choices to the user's own language choices, so they can understand the concepts more easily.
